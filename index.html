About

I'm a PhD Computer Science student at the University of Oxford, working on AI interpretability and control at the PIXL Lab, supervised by Prof. Ronald Clark.

My research explores understanding, monitoring and controlling large language models' behaviours, actions and latent dynamics, from understanding emergence and detecting signs of misalignment and reward hacking, to surfacing models' internal computations in reasoning faithfully, white-box auditing of models, safety training and scalable oversight.

In the past, I contracted at Anthropic working on post-training LLMs for generating robust reasoning, at METR contributing to AI R&D evals, at the UK AI Security Institute building multi-agent safety evaluations, and at Cadenza Labs training probes for detecting deception in models.
